<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/jumpy-ball1449/1">Jumpy Ball</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a jumpy ball that is thrown vertically upward from the ground to a height of <strong>h</strong>, it rebounds to a height of <strong>floor(h / 2)</strong>&nbsp;every time it hits the ground. The rebounding process continues until the rebound height becomes <strong>0</strong>. Return the total distance traveled by the ball.</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>h = 10<strong>
Output: </strong>36<strong>
Explanation: </strong>The ball travels: 10 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 36.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>h = 20<strong>
Output: </strong>76<strong>
Explanation: </strong>The ball travels: 20 + 20 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 76.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Constraints:</strong><br><span style="font-size: 18px;">0 ≤ h ≤ 5 * 10<sup>8</sup></span></span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Mathematics</code>&nbsp;
