<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/maximum-bitonic-subarray-sum5616/1">Maximum Bitonic Subarray Sum</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given an array <strong>arr[]</strong> of integers, find the maximum sum of a bitonic<strong> </strong>subarray.&nbsp; </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">A bitonic subarray is one in which the elements first increase, may stay the same, and then decrease. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">A strictly increasing or strictly decreasing subarray is also considered a bitonic subarray.</span><br><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong><br></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [5, 3, 9, 2, 7, 6, 4]</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Output:</strong> 19</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Explanation: </strong>The subarray with the maximum sum is [2, 7, 6, 4] with a sum of 19.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [3, 1, 8, 10, 10, 9, 9, 3]</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Output:</strong> 38</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Explanation: </strong>The bitonic subarray with the maximum sum is [1, 8, 10, 10, 9]. <br>The elements first strictly increase (1, 8, 10), stay the same at the peak (10, 10), and then strictly decrease (10, 9). </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Sum = 1 + 8 + 10 + 10 + 9 = 38.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [3, 1, 8, 10, 9, 9, 3]<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Output:</strong> 28</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Explanation: </strong></span><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">The bitonic subarray with the maximum sum is [1, 8, 10, 9]. The elements strictly increase (1, 8, 10) and then strictly decrease (10, 9). Sum = 1 + 8 + 10 + 9 = 28.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ arr.size() ≤ 10<sup>5</sup><br>1 ≤ arr[i] ≤ 10<sup>4</sup></span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Arrays</code>&nbsp;
